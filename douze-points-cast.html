Douze Points for Murder

Friday 6 November, evening

The countdown is on. The UK will soon announce its Eurovision entry, and two dazzling finalists, Mary and Suso (who happen to be married), are battling for the ticket. Everyone behind the bid has something riding on the result: money, reputations, careers... and secrets.

On the night before the big announcement, the talent scout is throwing a private party for the whole team. But not everyone's going to make it to the party. Somebody would kill for douze points.
